# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Resili a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology
choices.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, designs, and code rather than people.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they
determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers through the repository's issue or security contact
channels. Reports will be reviewed and investigated with appropriate
confidentiality.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.
